Faith sits at the centre of our lives. Being Muslim means more than having a Muslim name, dressing a certain way or coming from a Muslim family. It means knowing Allah, loving His Messenger ﷺ and trying to live by His guidance. Our faith answers life's biggest questions: where we came from, why we are here and where we are going. As young Muslims, we try to: - Anchor ourselves in worship. Build a strong bond with Allah through ṣalāh, the Qurʾān and sincere remembrance, starting with wuḍūʾ. Muslim engineers in the 13th century even built automated water dispensers for it. - Understand our beliefs. Learn what we believe in depth, and why. - Live the Sunnah. Love the Prophet ﷺ and follow his example, in big decisions and in small daily habits such as using the miswāk before prayer. - Stand firm. Stay confident in who we are when we are tested, remembering that Allah sees what we do in public and in private. We never feel apologetic or embarrassed about being Muslim. Our faith is something to treasure, understand and live by.
