A FINAL THOUGHT

WHAT WILL PEOPLE REMEMBER?

Not our grades or our prizes, but how we treated them, whether we kept our word, and whether we stood up for what was right.

Let us be a generation firm in faith, rich in knowledge, noble in character, confident in our identity and devoted to serving others.

Years from now, people may not remember our exam grades or the prizes we won. They will remember how we treated them: whether we kept our word, whether we stood up for what was right, and whether we made their lives better. Let us be a generation firm in faith, rich in knowledge, noble in character, confident in our identity and devoted to serving others. That is the standard we work towards every day at the Imam Muhammad Adam Institute.
