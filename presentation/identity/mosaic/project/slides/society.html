LEAD BY EXAMPLE

As Muslims in Britain, we are an active part of the society around us. Being a committed Muslim and contributing to British life support each other. As young British Muslims, we try to: - Walk with quiet confidence. Be proud of being Muslim, without cutting ourselves off and without compromising. - Build bridges. Respect people of all faiths and backgrounds and make real friendships, while staying true to our principles. - Stand up for justice. Take responsibility for our local community and speak up for fairness, even when it is unpopular. - Lead by example. Let our courtesy, honesty and warmth show people what Islam looks like. We don't watch from the sidelines. Rooted in our faith, we step forward as trustworthy neighbours, thoughtful citizens and people who work for the common good.
