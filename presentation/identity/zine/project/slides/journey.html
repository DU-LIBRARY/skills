LONDON

Yemeni coffee reaches the “penny universities”

When the Prophet ﷺ established the community of Madīnah, Muslims lived alongside neighbours of other faiths under clear agreements on rights, justice and shared responsibility. Muslim merchants, navigators and thinkers connected Africa, Asia and Europe. They exchanged ideas, improved navigation, spread crops such as citrus fruits, apricots and rice, shared water systems like qanats and irrigation rules that left their mark on Spain, and carried Yemeni coffee around the world, all the way to London's coffeehouses, the "penny universities".
