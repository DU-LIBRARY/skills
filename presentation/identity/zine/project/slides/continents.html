Men and women of many backgrounds built on earlier discoveries and pushed knowledge further.

Islam has given us a remarkable history, a rich intellectual heritage and, above all, a way of life with purpose and direction. From Spain and North Africa, through the Middle East, to Indonesia and China, Muslim men and women of many backgrounds built on the discoveries of earlier civilisations, invented new things and pushed human knowledge further. Our aim is to become principled Muslims whose knowledge, character and actions benefit the people around us.
