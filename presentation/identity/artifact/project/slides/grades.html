Our aim: Muslims whose faith, intellect and character benefit Britain and humanity.

As students of the Imam Muhammad Adam Institute, we are here for more than exams, good grades or a good career. We are here to strengthen our faith, gain knowledge, build good character and prepare for the responsibilities ahead of us. Islam has given us a remarkable history, a rich intellectual heritage and, above all, a way of life with purpose and direction. From Spain and North Africa, through the Middle East, to Indonesia and China, Muslim men and women of many backgrounds built on the discoveries of earlier civilisations, invented new things and pushed human knowledge further. Our aim is to become principled Muslims whose knowledge, character and actions benefit the people around us. Six qualities describe who we are and who we want to be.
