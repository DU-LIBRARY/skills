A LIFE OF SERVICE

الخدمة

وَأَحْسِنْ كَمَا أَحْسَنَ اللَّهُ إِلَيْكَ

“And do good as Allah has done good to you.”

Sūrat al-Qaṣaṣ: 77

Care deeply

Serve first

Solve real problems

Lead by serving

Islam measures greatness by what we give to others, not by what we collect for ourselves: وَأَحْسِنْ كَمَا أَحْسَنَ اللَّهُ إِلَيْكَ "And do good as Allah has done good to you." (Sūrat al-Qaṣaṣ: 77) In classical Muslim societies, faith led to institutions for people's welfare: public hospitals (bīmāristāns) with specialist wards, teaching hospitals and early mental health care, such as the hospital of Aḥmad ibn Ṭūlūn in Egypt, along with regulated pharmacies, water-raising systems that brought clean water to farms and towns, and public gardens. Each of us has our own talents and opportunities. Whether we become ʿulamāʾ, teachers, doctors, engineers, business owners or leaders in other fields, our abilities are blessings meant to help others. As students, we try to: - Care deeply. Feel real concern for people who are struggling, disadvantaged or overlooked. - Serve first. Take responsibility for our families, neighbourhoods and wider society. - Solve real problems. Use our skills to challenge injustice and make practical changes. - Rethink leadership. True leadership is khidmah, service, never ego or status. Success isn't counted in money, titles or trophies. It is measured by the lives made better because we were here.
