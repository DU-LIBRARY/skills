SEEKERS OF KNOWLEDGE

العلم

اقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ

“Read in the name of your Lord Who created.”

Sūrat al-ʿAlaq: 1

Love all useful learning

Question and reflect

Honour our heritage

Islam encourages curiosity, reflection and the courage to think. The first word revealed to the Prophet ﷺ was a command to learn: اقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ "Read in the name of your Lord Who created." (Sūrat al-ʿAlaq: 1) They did all this with parchment, ink and hard work. We have every book, laboratory and digital tool at our fingertips. So why shouldn't the next great doctor, engineer, mathematician, researcher or scholar be sitting in this room? As students, we try to: - Love all useful learning. Bring the same energy to the Islamic sciences and to our school subjects. - Question and reflect. Think critically, ask deep questions and aim to understand, not just memorise. - Honour our heritage. Respect our teachers, and treat knowledge as an amānah, a trust to use responsibly. Knowledge without character leads to arrogance. Knowledge guided by faith and humility benefits everyone.
