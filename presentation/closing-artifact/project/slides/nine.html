From the letter we can draw out nine principles: 1. A uniting factor 2. Equal citizenship in a joint state 3. Choosing the lesser evil 4. Constitutional and legal protections 5. Local and regional autonomy 6. Educational freedom and civic engagement 7. Continuous moral and civic effort 8. Principled foreign and public policy 9. Conditional cooperation
