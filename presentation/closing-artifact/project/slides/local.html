Madanī’s principles

Part two · Britain

BRITAIN · PRINCIPLES 5–7

٥

Local autonomy

Councils, school governors, mosque committees, forums

No provinces: our autonomy is local.

٦

Education

Faith schools and maktabs, within the rules

And an active part in state schools and universities.

٧

Moral effort

Politics never replaces Iṣlāḥ

Mentoring the young, strong families, honest conduct.

Fifth, local autonomy. We have nothing like Madanī's five Muslim-majority provinces. Our autonomy is local: councils, school governing bodies, mosque committees, neighbourhood forums and voluntary groups, where communities shape local services and cultural life. Sixth, education. Parents and teachers can set up faith schools and supplementary maktabs within the regulations, and play an active part in state schools, colleges and universities. Seventh, moral and civic effort. Political engagement never replaces Iṣlāḥ, inner reform. That means investing in mentoring young people, in strong families and in honest conduct in everyday life.
