Part two · Britain

THE LESSER EVIL

No Islamic state, but no withdrawal either

Vote and take part in public life, even when no party is perfect.

٤

LEGAL PROTECTIONS

Use and defend what the law already gives

Equality law · charity and waqf rules · protection for mosques, cemeteries and family practice.

Third, the lesser evil. We can't expect an Islamic state in Britain. But we can't afford to step back from politics either, and leave decisions about our schools, the economy and our basic freedoms to other people. Taking part in elections and public life, even when no party is perfect, protects our communities far better than withdrawing. Fourth, legal protections. We rely on, and defend, the safeguards UK law already gives us: equality and anti-discrimination law, the rules on charities and waqfs, and protection for mosques, Muslim cemeteries and the family practices British law allows.
