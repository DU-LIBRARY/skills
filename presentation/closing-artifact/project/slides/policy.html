FOREIGN AND PUBLIC POLICY

Vote, write to MPs, join the debate

Fair policies at home; a humane foreign policy and aid abroad.

CONDITIONAL COOPERATION

Wholehearted on the common good. Firm on faith.

The NHS, the cost of living, local services: together. Our principles and institutions: never traded.

Eighth, foreign and public policy. By voting, writing to MPs and taking part in public debate, British Muslims can argue for fair policies at home, and for a humane foreign policy and humanitarian aid abroad. Ninth and last, conditional cooperation. Madanī's lesson for us is to work wholeheartedly with the rest of society on the common good, such as the NHS, the cost of living and local services, while holding firm to our religious principles, our institutions and our moral limits.
