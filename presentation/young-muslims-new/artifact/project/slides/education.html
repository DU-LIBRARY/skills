As students of The Imam Muhammad Adam Institute, our education is about far more than examinations, qualifications or careers. It is about nurturing our faith, enriching our minds, refining our character and preparing ourselves to make a meaningful contribution to the world we live in. So, what kind of young Muslims should we aspire to become? Six foundational pillars will help us answer this question and shape the identity we strive to embody.
