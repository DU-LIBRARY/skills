Seekers of Knowledge · The first revelation

ALLAH TAʿĀLĀ SAYS

ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ

“Read in the name of your Lord Who created.”

SŪRAT AL-ʿALAQ 96:1

11

The very first revelation to the Prophet ﷺ began with the command: “Read in the name of your Lord Who created.” (Sūrat al-ʿAlaq: 1) Throughout history, Muslims took this command seriously. They dedicated their lives to learning, asked questions, examined ideas and made remarkable contributions to human knowledge.
