We were created by Allah, not without purpose, but to recognise Him as our Creator and live as His servants. Allah reminds us in the Qurʾān: “I did not create jinn and mankind except to worship Me.” (Sūrat al-Dhāriyāt, 51:56) Our purpose in life, therefore, is far greater than chasing wealth, status or worldly pleasures. We are here to worship Allah, earn His pleasure and prepare for the day we return to Him. And this is what makes Islam a complete way of life. Our faith is not confined to the masjid or the prayer mat. It should guide our words, shape our character, influence our friendships and govern our decisions, whether we are in public or alone. From how we treat our parents and teachers to how we conduct ourselves online, from the company we keep to the ambitions we pursue, our faith should be the guiding principle in everything we do.
