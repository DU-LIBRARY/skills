Noble in Character · Ask ourselves

19

WE SHOULD THEREFORE

Ask ourselves

i

Do we remain respectful when someone disagrees with us?

ii

Can we control our anger when someone insults or provokes us?

iii

Do we speak the truth, even when it may lead to consequences?

iv

Do we treat younger pupils and the less fortunate with kindness and dignity?

v

Can we admit our mistakes, apologise sincerely and accept correction?

vi

Do we stand up for someone being bullied, even when others stay silent?

Our true character is revealed when we are challenged, when we are angry, when we make mistakes and when doing the right thing requires courage. We should therefore ask ourselves: Do we remain respectful when someone disagrees with us? Can we control our anger when someone insults or provokes us? Do we speak the truth, even when it may lead to consequences? Do we treat younger pupils and those less fortunate with kindness and dignity? Can we admit our mistakes, apologise sincerely and accept correction? Do we stand up for someone being bullied, even when others remain silent? These are the moments in which our Islamic character is truly put to the test.
