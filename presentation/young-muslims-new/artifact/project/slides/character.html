3. Noble in Character: Our Identity of Adab and Akhlāq The true measure of our education is not simply what we know, but the kind of people we become. As young Muslims, our character should be one of our most distinguishing qualities. We should be recognised not only for our knowledge and achievements, but also for our honesty, humility, kindness and respect towards others.
