THE VALUE OF OUR YOUTH

اغْتَنِمْ خَمْسًا قَبْلَ خَمْسٍ: شَبَابَكَ قَبْلَ هَرَمِكَ

“Take advantage of five things before five: your youth before your old age.”

REPORTED BY AL-ḤĀKIM AND AL-BAYHAQĪ

The Value of Our Youth The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ gave us an important reminder about making the most of our youth: “Take advantage of five things before five: your youth before your old age.” (Reported by al-Ḥākim and al-Bayhaqī.) Youth is a precious blessing from Allah, a time of opportunity, growth and responsibility. The choices we make today will shape the individuals we become tomorrow.
