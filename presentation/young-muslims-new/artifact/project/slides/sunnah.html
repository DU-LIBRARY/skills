Noble in Character · The Sunnah

THE PROPHET ﷺ SAID

إِنَّمَا بُعِثْتُ لِأُتَمِّمَ مَكَارِمَ الْأَخْلَاقِ

“I was only sent to perfect righteous character.”

AL-BAYHAQĪ

أَكْمَلُ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ إِيمَانًا أَحْسَنُهُمْ خُلُقًا

“The most complete of the believers in faith are those who possess the best character.”

SUNAN AL-TIRMIDHĪ

17

The Prophet ﷺ himself explained the importance of character when he said: “I was only sent to perfect righteous character.” He also taught us: “The most complete of the believers in faith are those who possess the best character.” (Sunan al-Tirmidhī) These teachings remind us that good character is not simply an additional quality of a Muslim. It is an essential part of our faith and a reflection of the strength of our īmān.
