1. Strong in Faith: Our Islamic Identity As Muslims, our faith must lie at the heart of our lives. Being born into a Muslim family is a great blessing, but our Islam must be more than an inherited identity. It must be a faith we understand, cherish and strive to live by. Growing up in Britain, we will encounter different beliefs, contrasting world views and challenges to our convictions. We must therefore ground ourselves in sound Islamic knowledge, strengthen our īmān and develop an unwavering commitment to the Qurʾān and Sunnah. Our ethnicity, nationality and background may be part of who we are, but it is our faith that gives us our true purpose and direction in life.
