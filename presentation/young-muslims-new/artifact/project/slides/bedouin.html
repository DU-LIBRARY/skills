The Beautiful Character of the Prophet ﷺ The life of the Prophet ﷺ provides countless examples of kindness, patience, humility and forgiveness. On one occasion, the Prophet ﷺ was walking with Sayyidunā Anas ibn Mālik (raḍiya Allāhu ʿanhu) when a Bedouin approached him. The man seized the Prophet’s cloak and pulled it so forcefully that it left a mark on his neck. He then demanded that the Prophet ﷺ give him some of Allah’s wealth. How did the Prophet ﷺ respond? He turned towards the man, smiled and instructed that he be given something. (Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī and Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim) Consider the beauty of this response. Despite being treated with such disrespect, the Prophet ﷺ did not retaliate or allow anger to dictate his behaviour. Instead, he responded with patience, dignity and generosity. This is the character that our beloved Prophet ﷺ demonstrated, and it is the character we should strive to develop within ourselves. His greatness was reflected not only in his words, but in his everyday dealings with people. He treated the young and elderly with compassion, assisted his family with humility, forgave those who wronged him and stood firmly for justice.
