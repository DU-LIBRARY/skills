ALLAH TAʿĀLĀ SAYS

وَمَنْ أَحْسَنُ قَوْلًا مِّمَّن دَعَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَعَمِلَ صَٰلِحًا وَقَالَ إِنَّنِى مِنَ ٱلْمُسْلِمِينَ

“And who is better in speech than one who calls towards Allah, does righteous deeds, and says: Indeed, I am among the Muslims?”

SŪRAT FUṢṢILAT 41:33

Allah Taʿālā tells us in the Noble Qurʾān: “And who is better in speech than one who calls towards Allah, does righteous deeds, and says: Indeed, I am among the Muslims?” (Sūrat Fuṣṣilat, 41:33) This āyah beautifully captures the essence of what it means to be a Muslim. Our identity is not defined merely by our names, the clothes we wear, or the families we come from. It is rooted in our faith, reflected in our character, and demonstrated through our actions. It means living by the teachings of Islam, inviting others towards goodness with wisdom, and confidently embracing who we are as Muslims. Our faith should shape not only what we believe, but also how we think, how we behave, how we treat others, and the contributions we make to the world around us. Islam is not simply an identity we inherit, but a responsibility we must strive to live up to. So, what does this mean for us as young Muslims today? What kind of individuals should we aspire to become, and how can we carry forward the remarkable legacy of those who came before us?
