Noble in Character · The Qurʾān

ALLAH TAʿĀLĀ SAYS

وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٍ

“And indeed, you are surely upon
an outstanding standard of character.”

SŪRAT AL-QALAM 68:4

16

Our greatest example is the Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ, whose noble character Allah praises in the Qurʾān: “And indeed, you are surely upon an outstanding standard of character.” (Sūrat al-Qalam: 4)
