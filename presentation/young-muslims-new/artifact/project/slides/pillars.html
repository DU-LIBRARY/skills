So, what kind of young Muslims should we aspire to become? Six foundational pillars will help us answer this question and shape the identity we strive to embody. 1. Strong in Faith (al-Īmān) 2. Seekers of Knowledge (al-ʿIlm) 3. Noble in Character (al-Akhlāq) 4. Confident in Society (al-Mujtamaʿ) 5. Committed to Service (al-Khidmah) 6. Striving for Excellence (al-Iḥsān)
