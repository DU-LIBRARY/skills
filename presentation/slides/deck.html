Muttaḥidah QawmiyyatThe proofs · Qur’an
Relations with non-Muslims

Four degrees of closeness

muwālātمُوَالَاة

Love and close friendship from the heart. Kept for Muslims.

muwāsātمُوَاسَاة

Sympathy, charity and consolation. Permitted, unless they are at war with Muslims.

mudārātمُدَارَاة

Courtesy and friendliness. Permitted with all.

muʿāmalātمُعَامَلَات

Trade, partnership and employment. Permitted, if it does not harm Muslims.

Muttaḥidah QawmiyyatThe proofs · Sunnah
The proof from the Sunnah

The Constitution of Madinah

Drawn up by the Prophet ﷺ, it made Muslims and non-Muslims of Madinah one community.

Article 1
إنهم أمة واحدة من دون الناس

“They are one community, distinct from other people.”

Article 15
يهود بني عوف أمة مع المؤمنين
لليهود دينهم وللمسلمين دينهم

“The Jews of Banū ʿAwf are one community with the believers: the Jews have their religion, the Muslims theirs.”

Article 8
من تبعنا من يهود فإن له النصر والأسوة

“Jews who follow us are owed help and support.”
