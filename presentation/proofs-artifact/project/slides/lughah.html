THE LINGUISTIC PROOF

What the dictionaries say

Mukhtār al-Ṣiḥāḥ · al-Qāmūs · Tāj al-ʿArūs

قَوۡم

١

Men, excluding women

٢

Men, with women included

٣

Both men and women

None of the three separates Muslims from non-Muslims.

Linguistic Proofs: From multiple Arabic dictionaries such as Mukhtaar al-Sihaah, Al-Qamus, Taj al-Urus and others, we can conclude that qawm has three meanings in the Arabic language: 1) A group of men, excluding women. 2) A group consisting of men, where women are automatically included 3) A group comprising both men and women As you can see, none of these definitions differentiate between muslims and non-muslims. in light of these definitions let us look at the proofs for Muttahidah Qaumiyyah from the Qur'an and Sunnah.
