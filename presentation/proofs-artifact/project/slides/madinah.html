THE PROOF FROM THE SUNNAH

The Constitution of Madinah

Drawn up by the Prophet ﷺ, it made the Muslims and non-Muslims of Madinah one community.

ARTICLE 1

إنهم أمة واحدة من دون الناس

“They are one community, distinct from other people.”

ARTICLE 15

يهود بني عوف أمة مع المؤمنين
لليهود دينهم وللمسلمين دينهم

“The Jews of Banū ʿAwf are one community with the believers: the Jews have their religion, the Muslims theirs.”

ARTICLE 8

من تبعنا من يهود فإن له النصر والأسوة

“Jews who follow us are owed help and support.”

Proofs from the Sunnah: I would like to address one specific part of the sunnah that is one of the clearest proofs for the theory of composite nationalism, and that is the Constitution of Madinah drawn up by the Prophet. The fact that the constitution was even drawn up establishes the fact that muslims can make agreements with non-muslims in dar al-harb or dar al-aman. And further, some articles explicitly unite muslims and non-muslims as one nation. The agreement begins with: بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم In the name of Allah, the Most Compassionate and Most Merciful هذا كتاب من محمد النبى (رسول الله - صلى الله عليه وسلم) بين المؤمنين والمسلمين من قريش وأهل يثرب ومن اتبعهم فلحق بهم وجاهد معهم This is a writing of Prophet Mohammad (the Messenger of Allah - صلى الله عليه و آله وسلم); between the Believers and Muslims of Quraish and Yathrib (Madina), and those who follow them and are attached to them, and who fight together with them in war. Article 1: إنهم أمة واحدة من دون الناس They are one community to the exclusion of (other) people Article 15: وأن يهود بني عوف أمة مع المؤمنين لليهود دينهم وللمسلمين دينهم مواليهم وأنفسهم إلا من ظلم أو أثم فإنه لا يوتغ إلا نفسه وأهل بيته The Jews of the Banu Awf are one community with the believers. The Jews are free to practice their religion and the Muslims their religion. (This applies) to their clients and to themselves with the exception of anyone who has done wrong or committed treachery, for he harms only himself and his family. Many other tribes were also mentioned thereafter as part of this agreement. Furthermore, not only did this agreement permit assisting and sympathising with non-muslims, it even guaranteed to non-muslims the assistance of muslims: Article 8: وأنه من تبعنا من يهود فإن له النصر والأسوة غير مظلومين ولا متناصر عليهم Whoever of the Jews follow is will receive help and sympathy, without being wronged, and without one helping another against them
