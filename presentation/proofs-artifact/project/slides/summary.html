Muttaḥidah Qawmiyyat

The proofs · Summary

IN SUM

Muslims and non-Muslims can be one qawm

اللغة

Language

No meaning of qawm separates Muslim from non-Muslim.

القرآن

Qur’an

Peoples who rejected their prophets are still “his qawm”; only muwālāt is kept for Muslims.

السنة

Sunnah

Madinah’s constitution made one community, each with its own religion.

To sum up, the word qaum in the Arabic language can mean a group of men excluding women or a group of men where women are included automaticall or a group of men and women. The Qur'an uses the word qaum for disbelievers and attributes them to their respective prophets, showing that muslims and non-muslims can be called one qaum. The Qur'an also clarifies the different relationships muslims can have with non-muslims, namely 1) muwasat, showing sympathy, kindess and giving support 2)mudarat, acting with them in a couteous manner and 3) mu'amalat, conducting dealings and transactions with them. It also establishes the superiority of Islamic brotherhood to national brotherhood by preserving muwalat, a close relationship coming from the heart, solely for muslims. And from the sunnah, the constitution of Madinah bound all muslims and non-muslims who were part of the agreement as one qaum.
