Qur'anic Proofs: The word qaum appears over 200 times in the Qur'an. The main proofs come from when the word qaum is affixed to a pronoun, whether it be 3rd person كَذَّبَتۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحٍ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ The people of Nūḥ rejected the messengers or 2nd person وَأُوحِيَ إِلَىٰ نُوحٍ أَنَّهُۥ لَن يُؤۡمِنَ مِن قَوۡمِكَ إِلَّا مَن قَدۡ ءَامَنَ فَلَا تَبۡتَئِسۡ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَفۡعَلُونَ And it was revealed to Nūḥ: None of your people would come to believe any more, except those who have already believed. So, be not distressed by what they have been doing. or 1st person وَإِذۡ قَالَ مُوسَىٰ لِقَوۡمِهِۦ يَٰقَوۡمِ لِمَ تُؤۡذُونَنِي وَقَد تَّعۡلَمُونَ أَنِّي رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ إِلَيۡكُمۡۖ And (remember) when Mūsā said to his people, "O my people, why do you hurt me, while you know that I am a messenger of Allah sent towards you." And there are many such examples like these. In all these verses, the non-muslims who rejected their respective prophets have been called the qaum of said prophet. So if it is not belief that binds people together as one qaum, what else can it be besides sharing the same land, language, kinship or professional ties?
